What the plate shows

iThe archive is silted, correctly

Eighteen of twenty-six holdings in 40 Archives sit at the censor, untouched for the whole life of the record. In an archive that is the intended behaviour rather than decay. It is the one coast that should read this way.

iiThe unreachable coast is Recruiting

Seven of eleven notes in Recruiting, sixty-four percent of the territory, link to nothing and are linked from nothing. The coast is worked within days, yet most of it cannot be reached from any other shore.

iiiThe stemma confirms the boot order

Identity carries sixty-one backlinks, more than any other note, exactly as the documented Tier 0 claims. Instruction layer and link graph agree, which was not the expected result.

ivOne capital is undeclared

AXE_OS Governing Spec ties for the fourth largest port at forty-two backlinks and appears nowhere in the documented boot sequence. The brain leans on it harder than its own instructions admit.

vOne lane runs both ways

90 Meta sends one hundred and nine sailings to 30 Resources and receives ninety-one back, the only heavy route in near balance. The next two heaviest lanes, from Daily and from Projects, both run toward Meta.

viHalf of all traffic crosses a border

1034 of 2,334 links leave their territory. A brain filed in folders but read as a graph: the folders describe where things sit, not how they are used.

On the reliability of this chart. Three instruments were tried and two rejected. File modification times report every territory as two days old, an artefact of the move out of iCloud rather than a fact about the vault. Frontmatter dates cover only eighty-five percent of notes. Git history is used here and is honest, but cannot see before 26 May 2026. Where a coast is drawn silted, it is at least that silted.
